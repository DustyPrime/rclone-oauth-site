隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

What this application is

This is a personal-use OAuth client for rclone. It is operated by a single individual for backing up and syncing their own files. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not offered commercially.

What data it accesses

The client requests the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) solely to read, upload, and manage files that belong to the account owner.

Where data goes

No data leaves the owner's own machines and their own Google Drive. There is no server, no database, no analytics, and no third-party sharing. Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive over HTTPS.

Credentials

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine with file permissions restricted to the owner. They are never transmitted anywhere except to Google's OAuth and Drive APIs.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
